Our Mission

We want to help create streets that are safer, more connected, and easier for everyone to use.

🚲 Better Bike Infrastructure

Expand and improve bike routes so people can travel through Tahoe safely and comfortably.

🚶 Safer Walking Routes

Improve sidewalks, crossings, and connections between neighborhoods, schools, and important destinations.

🚗 Safer Streets

Encourage street designs and traffic speeds that reduce conflicts between vehicles, cyclists, and pedestrians.

🏔️ A Better Tahoe Community

Support transportation improvements that make Tahoe more accessible while preserving the character of the community.
