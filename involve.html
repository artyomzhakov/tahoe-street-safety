Get Involved

Safer streets start with people who notice problems, share their experiences, and work together toward solutions.

Help Us Understand Tahoe's Streets

Whether you walk, bike, drive, or use a combination of transportation, you experience our streets every day. Your observations can help us identify where improvements are needed most.

Report a Safety Concern

Have you encountered a dangerous crossing, missing bike infrastructure, poor visibility, or another street safety concern?

Tell us what you saw. Reports will help us identify patterns, document local problems, and determine where further research may be needed.

Submit a Community Report

Other Ways to Help

📸 Document

Photograph and document street conditions that make walking or biking difficult or unsafe.

🗺️ Explore

Use our Community Safety Map to identify areas that may need attention and see what others have reported.

🗣️ Share

Tell friends, neighbors, classmates, and other community members about the initiative.

💡 Contribute Ideas

Have an idea for making a street, crossing, or bike route safer? We want to hear it.

Help Build Something Bigger

Tahoe Street Safety Initiative is starting in Incline Village, but our long-term goal is to help create safer, more walkable, and more bikeable communities throughout the Tahoe Basin.

Community involvement will help determine where we go next.
