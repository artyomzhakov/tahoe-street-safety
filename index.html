Safer Streets for Tahoe

Working toward safer, more walkable, and more bike-friendly communities around Lake Tahoe.

Get Involved

Why This Matters

Communities around Lake Tahoe should be safe and accessible for everyone, whether they are walking, biking, driving, or using public transportation.

Safety

Safer crossings, slower vehicle speeds, and better infrastructure can help reduce crashes and protect people walking and biking.

Accessibility

Better sidewalks, bike routes, and crossings make it easier for people of all ages to get around the community.

Community

Walkable and bikeable streets create stronger connections between neighborhoods, businesses, schools, and public spaces.
