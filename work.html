Our Work

Turning local concerns into practical ideas for safer walking and biking in Incline Village and, eventually, communities throughout the Tahoe Basin.

Problem to Proposal

1

Document

Identify locations where people walking or biking face unsafe conditions. This can include missing bike infrastructure, difficult crossings, poor visibility, or roads where vehicle speeds create conflicts.

2

Research

Study traffic patterns, existing infrastructure, crash information, transportation plans, and successful safety improvements from other communities.

3

Develop Solutions

Turn our research into practical recommendations, focusing on improvements such as safer crossings, connected bike routes, and infrastructure that encourages safer vehicle speeds.

4

Build Awareness

Share what we learn with the community through our website, educational materials, photography, maps, and community feedback.

5

Bring Ideas Forward

When a proposal is well researched and supported, present it to relevant community organizations, agencies, and local decision-makers.

Where We're Starting

🚲 Safer Biking

Identify gaps in bike infrastructure and opportunities to create safer, more connected routes.

🚶 Safer Crossings

Find pedestrian crossings that could be made safer, clearer, and easier to use.

📍 Local Evidence

Use photographs, maps, observations, data, and community feedback to understand local problems.

Starting Local, Thinking Bigger

Our initial focus is Incline Village. As our research, community involvement, and experience grow, we hope to expand our work to other communities throughout the Tahoe Basin.
