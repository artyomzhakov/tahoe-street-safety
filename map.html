Community Safety Map

A growing map of locations in Incline Village and the Tahoe Basin where improvements to walking and biking safety may be needed.

Coming Soon

We are working toward creating an interactive map that documents community-reported street safety concerns.

As reports are collected and reviewed, the map will help identify patterns and highlight locations that may deserve further research.

Report a Safety Concern

What Will Appear on the Map?

🚲 Bike Safety

Locations with missing, disconnected, or difficult-to-use bike infrastructure.

🚶 Pedestrian Safety

Crossings, sidewalks, and other areas where walking conditions may need improvement.

⚠️ Reported Concerns

Community observations that can help identify locations for further investigation.

From Reports to Action

The map is intended to be more than a collection of pins. Over time, documented locations can help us understand recurring problems, research potential solutions, and develop more informed recommendations.
